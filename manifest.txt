MANDATORY PLACEMENT DECISION
----------------------------
A. New CLI behavior? Extend the relevant src/commands/ handler and src/program.ts only for true command registration changes.
B. Run or artifact lifecycle? src/run.ts, src/runLifecycle.ts, src/artifactLifecycle.ts, src/stageDetector.ts as applicable. Do not conflate a supplemental reference with a native artifact.
C. Readiness or eligibility? RunIntegrityGate is the policy owner; JudgeIntegrity consumes its expected verdict; correctionRouter owns valid correction targets.
D. Kit or Observer native evidence? Reuse their bounded owner/parser; ECO-00 cross-tool references must preserve native producer identities and exact supported schema versions. A separately approved new contract adapter may live in a distinct scoped src/ subdirectory with closest tests; no universal copied producer payload.
E. Instruction/packet/context? Existing src/instructions/ modules and their matched tests, never another global instruction registry.
F. Greenfield target/profile? Extend src/greenfield/<existing owner>/; no additional mode/stage/profile without roadmap authorization.
G. Text or structured native checking? Extend existing artifact/prompt/contract checking owner before adding a new validator.
H. Test? Put it next to closest established source owner in src/__tests__/, tests/, or tests/greenfield/ as current convention dictates; fixtures in tests/fixtures/ with provenance.
I. Public documentation? Update existing docs/<responsibility>.md. Planning-only reports remain ignored unless owner explicitly requests tracked plan.
J. Generated output? Approved ignored project-local roots, never src/, docs/, artifacts/, or historical tracked reports/.
K. New directory? Demonstrate missing existing owner, state intended API/contracts, closest tests, compatibility impact and approved version scope, then update manifest during the same authorized change.
